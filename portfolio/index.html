Heyside Group
Automation & Robotics 2026

Robotic Painting Cell

Through an Innovate UK Knowledge Transfer Partnership (KTP)[1] between Heyside Group and the University of Salford to implement plant-wide automation, I've been appointed to lead the project and identified critical opportunities for process optimization [2]. Targeting the painting process—which suffered from high paint waste and excessive labor costs—I designed and commissioned a complete robotic painting solution. This eliminated material waste, reduced labor costs, and delivered significantly higher coating quality and overall operational efficiency.

AMRC
Motion Control & Automation 2025

Motion Development Platform 2 (MDP2)

Adaptix reached out to develop their advancing portable, low-dose 3D X-ray imaging technology. Working alongside my colleagues at the AMRC, I supported the electrical and automation development for a prototype X-ray scanning machine. We implemented a split panel architecture using a slip ring with remote Beckhoff I/O, designed detailed electrical panel schematics in EPLAN, and implemented a Beckhoff TwinCAT control system to drive two servo-driven axes. The project helped providing the high-precision motion control required, accelerating Adaptix's technology development and prototype validation.

AMRC & Rolls-Royce
Quality Control & Automation 2025

MUSIC (Rolls-Royce) - Manufacturing Unequalled by Sustainable Innovation & Cost

In an Aerospace Technology Institute (ATI) funded project led by Rolls-Royce in collaboration with the AMRC, the goal was to develop an automated inspection system utilising machine vision to inspect Rolls-Royce Trent XWB airplane engine componenets. As the Machine Integration Engineer on the project, we delivered a 12-axis motion control system for the inspection cell. Working alongside the engineering team, I defined the complete control system architecture, authored the functional description document, conducted comprehensive risk assessments, designed compliant electrical schematics in EPLAN, and commissioned the multi-axis motion system using Siemens and MACH4 technology. This collective effort successfully delivered a high-precision, fully compliant inspection platform, allowing Rolls-Royce to evaluate and deploy advanced automated machine vision inspection techniques within their manufacturing facilities.

AMRC
Digital Twin & Virtual Commissioning 2025

De-Risking Virtual Commissioning for Robotic Cells

Funded by AMRC tier-1 industrial members including Boeing, Airbus, and BAE Systems—this project evaluated how virtual simulation platforms and digital twins (such as Visual Components) can reduce development time and costs compared to direct physical commissioning. Working alongside the engineering team, I designed the Beckhoff TwinCAT 3 control system, created electrical panel schematics in EPLAN, integrated KUKA KRC5 and ABB IRC5 robots via EtherCAT and DeviceNet gateways, and established real-time OPC UA communication with Visual Components. Our team successfully completed the cell, validated the digital twin concept, and reported the findings directly to the industrial partners, proving that virtual commissioning effectively mitigates physical integration risks and accelerates deployment

AMRC & Mitsubishi
Predictive Maintenance & AI 2024

Tool Condition Monitoring (TCM) with Mitsubishi Heavy Industries

In a targeted project with Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (MHI) to detect tool wear in industrial milling applications, my team and I were tasked with developing real-time Tool Condition Monitoring (TCM) technologies. Serving as Lead Engineer, I collaborated with the Process Monitoring & Control team to define project requirements, design and execute extensive machining trials to capture multi-sensor data, and engineer an NMFA-based diagnosis algorithm to detect flank wear. We successfully validated the algorithm's performance for real-time application scenarios and provided MHI with technical recommendations for future deployment.

AMRC
Predictive Maintenance & AI 2023

Autonomous Tool Anomaly Detection

For a ~£1M EPSRC-funded research initiative in collaboration with industrial partners including Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, the objective was to develop an autonomous diagnostic tool for machine and cutting tool health. Working alongside the research team, I led experimental validation and data modeling by conducting machining trials on a spindle test rig and CNC machines at the AMRC. I applied novel Sensor Data Modelling and Model Frequency Analysis (SDMMFA), engineered multi-sensor features, and built AI models for anomaly detection, successfully validating the technology and collaborating with industrial partners to plan its real-world manufacturing deployment.

Sheffield Hallam University
Swarm Robotics 2023

IDs Assignments in Multi robot systems

As a requiuermnt for my Msc dissertation, and in the context of swarm robotics, effective robot identification and coordination are critical for mission success. I developed a novel ID assignment algorithm to address the challenge of efficiently assigning unique identifiers to a group of robots within a shared environment. This algorithm enables seamless communication and collaboration among robots, ensuring that each unit can be unambiguously identified and controlled. The approach was validated through simulations, demonstrating its effectiveness in dynamic multi-robot scenarios and providing a foundation for future swarm intelligence applications.

Holcim
Process Control 2021

Karsta, Material Handling Control System

I was proud to have contributed to the launch of KARASTA,it was a new product line for Holcim cement plant in Iraq, supporting the programming, commissioning, and operation of a new material transportation system comprising conveyor belts, bins, weigh feeders, Siemens ET 200 remote I/O, and motor control centres (MCCs). My responsibility in this project was on developing and integrating Rockwell ControlLogix PLCs and FactoryTalk HMI systems to ensure reliable operation and smooth production. Working closely with the process and production teams, we delivered all automation activities in-house, avoiding external contractors, reducing project costs, and strengthening internal technical expertise while improving process quality and production efficiency.

Holcim
MPC and Advanced Control 2021

Mill Master, High level Control System (HLC)

Aiming to maximize throughput and energy efficiency in cement milling operations, our team sought to implement intelligent high-level control into the existing plant automation architecture. I integrated a smart fuzzy logic control system developed in LabVIEW directly into the plant's main Allen-Bradley PLCs using OPC communications for real-time data exchange. This integration successfully enabled automated, real-time optimization of cement milling processes, stabilizing mill loads and improving production consistency.

Holcim
Process Automation 2020

Water Station Rehabilitation

To modernise critical plant infrastructure at the Holcim cement plant located in Iraq, my team and I were responsible for overhauling and automating the facility's water treatment and distribution station. Working with the plant engineering team, I engineered and commissioned a new PLC-based control system using Allen-Bradley (Rockwell ControlLogix) controllers. that interfaced with Siemens Simocode motor control units, VFDs and remote I/O panels over Profibus DP, developed PID loops for the pumps and control the screens in plant SCADA system for the operators, delivering a resilient automation system aligned with global efficiency and international safety standards.

Holcim
AI Predictive Modeling 2019

Concrete Strength Prediction System

To eliminate delays associated with traditional destructive compressive strength testing, my team and I developed a system to estimate concrete quality dynamically during production. Working alongside process engineers, I implemented AI-driven predictive models that evaluated real-time operational and raw material parameters directly from the plant control system. This delivered accurate, real-time compressive strength estimates, allowing operators to make immediate quality control adjustments.
